# VoyageGraph sample requests

Use exact cities and ISO dates for the strongest live results.

## Complete trip

> Plan a five-day comfort trip from Indore to Goa for two adults. Depart on 2026-10-10 and
> return on 2026-10-14. Keep the total budget near INR 80,000. We enjoy beaches, local food,
> photography, and relaxed mornings. Compare outbound and return flights, hotels, weather,
> itinerary, and budget.

## Flight comparison

> Compare useful one-way flights from DEL to BOM on 2026-10-15 for one adult. Prefer direct
> flights and clearly separate verified prices from anything unavailable.

## Hotel research

> Find suitable hotels in Jaipur from 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-23 for two adults, below INR 7,000
> per night. Prioritize strong ratings and central locations.

## Follow-up questions

- Make the itinerary less rushed and keep every afternoon flexible.
- Replace the most expensive hotel with a budget alternative.
- Which parts of this plan were verified with live tools?
- Recalculate the plan for three travelers.
- Give me a packing checklist based only on the returned weather forecast.
